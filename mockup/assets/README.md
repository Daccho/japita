`python mockup/refresh-preview.py` embeds the WebP files into the marked image block in `japita-workspace.html`, then regenerates `index.html`. The fragment and standalone preview use the same embedded images without changing the sandbox or CSP. `--check` verifies that the embedded assets and preview match their source files.

```text

Asset type: edited Japita brand logo for a small restaurant purchasing app header.
Input image 1 is the existing Japita logo and the edit target. Preserve its recognizable shopping cart + chef hat + dollar motif and exact brand name "Japita" (J-a-p-i-t-a), but restyle it for the existing Ajitama presentation design: warm charcoal ink #1E1B18, flat printed graphics, confident simple shapes, Japanese restaurant editorial character, friendly geometric display lettering with the compact feel of Dela Gothic One. Make a cleaner, less bulky cart and chef-hat symbol, visually secondary to the readable wordmark. Use ONE color, warm charcoal; replace all navy and orange. No colored i dot.
Composition: a single compact horizontal lockup: a small cart/chef-hat symbol on the left and the full word Japita on the right, visually balanced, about 3.5 to 4 times as wide as tall. Optimize the silhouette and lettering to remain clear when the whole logo is shown roughly 100px wide and 28px tall. Wordmark may be restyled but must be spelled exactly Japita. Use an appropriately wide canvas, tightly frame the complete lockup with only a small even transparent margin. All five letters after J must be clearly separated and legible.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha everywhere outside the logo and inside its open spaces. No white rectangle, no badge, no backdrop, no shadow, no gradient, no distressed texture, no mockup, no decorative Japanese text, no slogan, no extra graphic or new motif, no watermark. Return only one finished horizontal logo, not a comparison sheet. Preserve the brand idea while adapting its drawing style and typography; do not merely desaturate the old bitmap.
```
