# Japita: Hackathon Build Plan

Working draft, 17 October 2026. The plan is split by topic under `docs/`; this file is the index.

**One line:** Japita centralizes a restaurant's suppliers, offers, purchasing, and spending, and advises on each week's basket across existing and new suppliers based on quality, delivered price, and the owner's preferences. The first demo is a Japanese ramen business in Milan during a normal purchasing week.
